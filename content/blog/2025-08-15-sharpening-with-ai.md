
## Key Points

- AI is good at helping with small things, brain storming, and troubleshooting
- AI is good at building small things from scratch
- AI is bad at building and refactoring big things
- AI doesn't make you more productive
- AI will lie to you
- People are sick of AI being shoved in their face
- Skill attrition ("Use it or lose it")
- Privacy nightmare
- Use it mindfully for targeted tasks
- You are ultimately responsible for the code the AI writes
- There is no "secret sauce" to make it work for you

Cases where AI is useful:

- Greenfield projects
- Building small things from scratch
- Writing code in unfamiliar domains
